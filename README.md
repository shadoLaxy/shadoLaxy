## ♣️About Me

I am an aspiring Indie Game Developer and as a programmer I have at heart:

- 🫧 <strong>Clean Architecture</strong> - For reusability and quick problem solving
- 📋 <strong>Player friendly UIs</strong> - Make the interface experience fluid and unique
- 💭 <strong>Think before Do</strong> - A simple plan on what to program before starting to write the code

I personally believe writing code is about having fun, making mistakes and solving them. Meanwhile making sure the program is clean, optimized, performative and reusable for future projects. That is another aspect I sincerely appreciate in programming, everything I do today is also something for the me of tomorrow. 

In my free time I usually watch anime, continue developing, drawing and writing for my stories, play video games or pass time with my friends and family.


## ♥️My Game Development Philosophy

For my games, I place a strong focus on:

- 🎮 <strong>Turn-Based RPGs</strong> - Strategic combats and meaningful decisions
- 🪶 <strong>Deep and Engaging Stories</strong> - Detailed story worthy experiencing
- 🎲 <strong>Fair RNG system</strong> - Reducing frustrating randomness without removing unpredictability 
- 🚫🤖 <strong>Human-Driven Art Direction</strong> - No AI generated Art or artistic direction 
- 🧩 <strong>Creative Game Mechanics</strong> - Unique ideas and fresh gameplay experience
- 💡<strong>Open for new Ideas</strong> - Always exploring new possibilities and inspiration
- 🔘<strong>Self Difficulty Choice</strong> - Decide if you want to play easy, medium or hard mode! 

As a gamer myself I know the frustration towards RNG (especially in gacha games) or Levelling up for the next boss. I don’t find this part necessarily negative, but I always loved when a game would at the very least appreciated my strategic thinking instead of only caring about ‘What level are you/your characters?’ or ‘Do you have specific X element for completing this challenge?‘. Obviously those mechanics will always have an impact on your playthrough, but I would honestly appreciate making games that take under consideration your personal thinking.
